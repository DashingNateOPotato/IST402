Organizational Compliance Framework Tool


Welcome to the Compliance Risk Framework Tool!

This tool will enable users to identify and recommend compliance laws, regulations, and controls.

Users will respond to a slew of questions in order to figure out what kind of organization they operate.

The questions will then be sent to an AI database, and then results will be sent back to us!

Once completed and submitted, the user will be directed to a Summary page to discover the results!
